Key Concepts

VOW Act

  • Prevents JVSG staff from performing non-veteran duties.
  • Enforced through audits, NVTI training, and testing.

DOL VETS Mission

  • Prepare
  • Provide
  • Protect
  • Promote

DVOP

  • Provides intensive services and case management.
  • Works with veterans with SBEs/QEBs.

LVER

  • Primary duty: Employer outreach.
  • Secondary duty: Staff training and capacity building.

Priority of Service

  • Veterans receive priority access to workforce services.
  • Requires 1 day active duty and discharge other than dishonorable.
